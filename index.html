06 · Leadership

Stewards of the standard.

Andre B. Audifferen

Managing Partner · Chief Executive Officer

Andre brings over ten years of experience in the Nigerian real estate industry, spanning development management, project coordination and delivery, end-to-end project strategy, value engineering, materials procurement, design conceptualisation, business development, land acquisition, statutory compliance, joint-venture acquisition, financial modelling, project funding, feasibility analysis, sales, marketing and company operations.

He served as Business Development Officer at a turnkey marketing, sales and PR firm whose portfolio included luxury developments, international real estate investments and an annual flagship real estate conference — before spending seven-plus years at Fort Knox Property Solutions Limited, first as Business Development Manager and later as Chief Operating Officer, overseeing all operational and strategic aspects of multiple developments.

His record includes high-value development delivery, raising project finance, closing joint-venture deals, large-scale sales campaigns, full-cycle project delivery, real estate research, and closing high-value sales and lettings.
